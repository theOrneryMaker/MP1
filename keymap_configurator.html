No drive folder
Opened from the CIRCUITPY drive, this page loads keymap.js from beside it automatically. Or start from scratch below, or open a keymap.js / keymap.json file. Version 1 files are converted automatically.

Save keymap

Save to drive puts keymap.js on the CIRCUITPY drive (start the macropad while holding the encoder so the drive appears and can be written). The macropad restarts by itself and uses it straight away. To copy it by hand, put keymap.js in the root of the drive. The text below is the plain JSON (what the copy button at its top right copies, and what Save as… writes for a .json name); keymap.js is the same JSON on one extra line.
Screen preview
As on the macropad's screen: icons cut to 4 characters, ERR on keys with a problem. Click a key to jump to it; drag one onto another to swap them. Stays up while you edit.
