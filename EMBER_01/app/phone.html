Plug the pad into the phone (a USB-C cable or an OTG adapter), then Connect… and allow MP1 to use it.

Update the pad

Puts MP1 Ember on the pad in one go, from any firmware. It saves a backup to the phone first, converts your keymap, secret layers and settings, keeps your wiring and other files, and puts the page (MP1 Ember.html) on the pad's drive.

A pad is in update mode (its RPI-RP2 drive). Finish with a backup made by this app: Ember and your files go back on.

Layers & keys

Share a layer or a key (Webex, email…), copy it, or save it to the phone. Secret pages are never shown here.
