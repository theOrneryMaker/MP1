Help

MP1 Ember: the pad and this page. Everything here works on Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Connecting

Plug the pad in and click Connect…, then pick its port. If the page says the port didn't answer, you picked the pad's console: click Connect… again and pick its other port. After the first time the page connects by itself.

Only one tab or program can use the pad at a time. Close the others if the page can't connect.

Theme (top right) picks the page's colors: Ember, OG (the original orange and purple), Ocean, Forest or Graphite, and Dark / Light / Auto (Auto follows the computer). It's remembered in this browser.

Macros from a spreadsheet (.csv): the guide

For long, repetitive entry: 50–100 order lines, or the same form filled again and again. You build the list in Excel, save it as a .csv file, and the page turns it into one macro key. Press the key and the pad types the whole list.

1. The idea: one header row, then your data

Row 1 says what to press after each column. Every row under it is one line of data, typed in turn.

Part number {TAB},Quantity {TAB 2},Unit {TAB 3},Plant {ENTER}{WAIT 0.5}
4471-220,12,EA,Plant 3
4471-221,6,EA,Plant 3

For each row the pad types 4471-220, presses Tab, types 12, presses Tab twice, types EA, Tab three times, types Plant 3, presses Enter, waits half a second, then starts the next row. The words before the braces (“Part number”) are only names for you: they're never typed. An empty cell types nothing but still presses its column's keys.

2. Two forms on one page

Both forms once per record (say 7 fields at the top, 5 further down): put all 12 columns side by side in one row. The header of the top form's last column holds the keys that get you to the bottom form, e.g. Notes {TAB 2}{WAIT 0.5}. Find them by clicking through the page once with only the keyboard and counting the Tabs.

The top form once, then many lines below it (an order's details, then its 50 lines): start a new section with a row whose first cell begins with {HEADER}. It's a header like row 1, with its own columns and keys, and the rows under it use it until the next {HEADER} row:

Customer {TAB},PO {TAB},Date {TAB},Ship via {TAB},Terms {TAB},Rep {TAB},Notes {TAB 2}{WAIT 0.5}
Acme Supply,PO-88213,11/24/2026,UPS,Net 30,JC,Dock 4
{HEADER}Part {TAB},Qty {TAB},Unit {TAB},Price {TAB},Plant {ENTER}{WAIT 1}
4471-220,12,EA,4.10,Plant 3
4471-221,6,EA,2.75,Plant 3
{HEADER}Finish
{CTRL+S}{WAIT 2}

Keys that run once, up front (say 49 presses of F7 to create a form's columns): give them a section of their own at the top: a header row (any name, e.g. Setup), then one row with the keys, e.g. {F7 49 0.2}{WAIT 1}, then {HEADER} for the form itself.

The last section is a single cell of keys: it saves the order once, after all the lines. A file can have as many sections as the page needs, and it's still one macro key. The preview lists each section with its rows and columns. The pad gets between fields with keys, not mouse clicks: if a form is only reachable with the mouse, look for a keyboard route first.

3. The keys you can use (in curly braces)

WritePresses
{TAB} {ENTER} {ESC} {SPACE}Tab, Enter, Escape, Space
{UP} {DOWN} {LEFT} {RIGHT}the arrow keys
{HOME} {END} {PGUP} {PGDN}Home, End, Page Up, Page Down
{DEL} {BACKSPACE} {INSERT}Delete, Backspace, Insert
{F1} … {F24}the function keys
{TAB 3} {DOWN 5}a key several times (up to 999), as fast as the pad can
{F7 49 0.2}49 presses, 0.2 s apart (for a page that needs a moment after each press)
{CTRL+S} {CTRL+SHIFT+V} {ALT+N} {WIN+R}keys held together (up to 4)
{WAIT 0.5}a pause, in seconds (up to 60)
{DATE} · {TIME}today's date (11/21/2026) / the time (2:05 PM), typed when the macro runs
{DATE YYYY-MM-DD}the date your way (YYYY, MM, DD, M, D, HH, hh, h, mm, AMPM)
{DATE MM/DD/YYYY +180}a date counted from today: +180 = 180 days ahead, -30 = 30 days ago ({DATE +90} uses 11/21/2026 style)
{NEXT LINE}the next number of the counter LINE: 1, then 2, 3… (any name; reset it on the pad: TOOLS → Counter)

Capitals or not, it doesn't matter: {tab} works too. Keys can also go inside any cell: a cell holding {CTRL+A}Plant 3 selects what's already in the field, then types over it.

4. Pauses: give the website time

The pad types far faster than a person. Wherever the website has to catch up, put a {WAIT …} after the key that makes it work: a drop-down list loading, a new order line appearing, a page saving. Start generous (0.5–1 s); once it runs cleanly, shorten them. The pauses in the header row happen on every row.

5. Build it in Excel

  • Type the header row in row 1, then paste your data below it.
  • Before pasting, format the columns as Text (select them, right-click → Format Cells → Text). Otherwise Excel turns 00123 into 123, part numbers like 1-2 into dates, and long numbers into 1.2E+11, and the pad types what Excel shows.
  • File → Save As → CSV (Comma delimited). Commas inside a cell are fine: Excel puts that cell in quotes.
  • The pad types the letters, numbers and symbols of a US keyboard. Accented letters or curly quotes (often pasted from email) can't be typed: the preview lists them with their row and column so you can fix them.

6. Put it on a key

  1. In Keys, pick a key, set its action to Macro, and click From a CSV…. (Or just drop the .csv file onto a key of the pad drawing.)
  2. Open your .csv, or paste the cells straight from Excel. The preview shows what follows each column, how many rows and steps, and roughly how long it takes to type. Anything it doesn't understand is listed with its row and column.
  3. Click Replace the macro (or Add to the end). It goes to the pad at once. Give the key a label you'll recognize.

7. Test it safely first

Open Notepad, click into it, and press the key. You see exactly what the website would get, with nothing at stake. Then click into the first field on the website and press the key for real. To stop a macro part way, press any key, turn the encoder or press it: it stops at once, lets go of everything, and the pad shows STOPPED. Nothing more is typed.

8. Share it

Send the .csv itself (anyone can open and change it in Excel), or the finished key: Copy key and paste it into Webex or an email; the other person selects a key and presses Ctrl+V. See “Sharing keys and layers” below.

Examples to start from

  • 6 order lines: part, quantity, unit, plant, Enter and a short wait per line.
  • a 5-field form per row: waits between fields, ↓ Enter to pick from a drop-down, Ctrl+S to save, Alt+N for a new form.
  • a 7-field form and a 5-field form further down, filled once per row (12 columns side by side).
  • the top form once, then 6 lines in the form below ({HEADER} sections), then one save.

The encoder

  • Turn: what the layer's encoder does (volume, scroll, …). Set it in Keys → Layer settings.
  • Click: Layer keys (or the layer picker, or Layer cards). Double click: the Spotify screen. Triple click: the home layer. Change them in Settings → Encoder.
  • Hold and press a key: the HOLD+KEY keys. Their screen (black on white) shows as soon as you hold; take your time.
  • Hold and turn: the Hold + turn functions in Settings → Encoder.
  • Hold 1.5 s and let go (no key, no turn): the TOOLS menu: Timer, Counter, Busy light, Settings, Mouse jiggle, Lock keys, Drive, About.
  • Layer cards: one big card a layer, its picture with the name below (or just the name, big): easy to read at arm's length. Turn to flip through them, click to pick. Give a layer its card picture in Keys → Layer settings → Layer card.
  • In a menu, the layer picker or Layer cards: turn to move, click to choose, hold to close. Settings are saved when the menu closes. A key press there only closes it: nothing is typed.
  • Locked: hold the encoder 3 s to unlock.

Keys

The middle shows the pad's screen, drawn exactly as the pad draws it. Click a key to edit it on the right:

  • Label: the words on the key. The page shows how they fit (one line next to a picture, two without).
  • Picture: an icon from four sets (search them; they load from the internet only while you choose), or draw one. With label keeps it small above the words; Alone, big fills the key. Smaller / Bigger adjust an icon.
  • Action: Keys (up to 4 together; Capture… reads them from your keyboard), Text, Macro, Media, Open, Go to a layer, a pad function, or the mouse. Repeat while held and Hold to confirm are below it.

Every change goes to the pad at once and is saved there. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. If the pad's drive is showing on the computer, the pad can't save its files: changes then last until it restarts.

Macros

Choose Macro as the action. Record… adds what you type: letters run together into one Text step, shortcuts and keys like Enter become Keys steps, and pressing the same key again counts it (×2, ×3…). Tick keep pauses as waits to keep your timing. Click Stop recording when done.

Edit any step afterwards, change its count, move it, remove it, or Add a step (Keys, Text, Wait, Media, Mouse, Open). A few keys never reach the page (Win alone sometimes, Alt+Tab, Ctrl+W): add those as steps.

Stopping a macro: while one runs, press any key, turn the encoder or press it. It stops at once (even in the middle of a wait or a long text), lets go of every key, and the pad shows STOPPED. That press does nothing else.

Sharing keys and layers

Copy key (under a key's settings) puts it on the clipboard as text: paste it into Webex or an email. Whoever gets it selects a key in their page and presses Ctrl+V. Save key… makes the same as a file (.mp1key.json, plain text); Load key…, or dropping the file on a key of the pad drawing, puts it on a key. Layers work the same way (Layer settings → Copy layer / Save layer…; Load layer… under the Layers list adds one). Undo takes a loaded key back. Keys and layers saved from the old MP1 page load too. Before you send one, check it holds nothing private.

Layers

The left column shows every layer; HOLD+KEY is last. Under the selected layer: Layer settings and Delete…. Drag a layer to move it, or use + Add layer.

Layer settings: the name, the home layer (the one the pad starts on), move, duplicate, delete, the encoder (label, turn right, turn left, speed-up), the layer's own lights, and mouse jiggle / Num Lock while it shows. Adding, renaming, moving or deleting shows SYNCING on the pad for a moment.

Secret pages

Up to four pages of keys the pad keeps to itself. The Secret pages pad function (hold + turn left to start with; put it on any gesture, key or HOLD+KEY key) opens page 1, and turning the encoder goes through the pages (the top row shows which, e.g. 1/2). On a secret page the encoder only turns pages: it sends nothing. The same function again closes them, and after the Secret lock time with no use (Settings → Other; 0 = never) they close by themselves. Secret page 2 (a pad function) opens page 2 straight away.

  • They're never in the pad's layer picker or Layer keys, and the player only ever shows “SECRET”, never their name or keys.
  • They're saved in secret.json, encrypted to this pad's own chip: a copy of the file can't be read anywhere else, not even by another MP1. The page can only open or change them with this pad on the cable.
  • In Keys they're at the bottom of the Layers list (“Secret page 1 · …”). Edit their keys like any layer; + Secret page adds one, and their Layer settings have Delete this secret page….
  • Converting a pad from the old firmware converts its Secret 1 and Secret 2 into pages 1 and 2; the old file is kept in the old_keymap folder.

Settings, Files, Player

  • Settings: the same settings as the pad's TOOLS menu, changed live. Default puts one back.
  • Files: what's on the pad's drive and how much room is left. Download any file; add or delete your own files. Light scenes (.mp1scene.json) go into scenes/ by themselves; restart the pad to use new ones. The firmware and the keymap files are protected here: change those in Keys and Settings.
  • Player: the pad's screen, live. Click a key to press it, or a layer to switch to it. Always on top puts it in a small window that stays above your other windows.

Timer, counter, date and busy light

  • Timer / stopwatch: TOOLS → Timer (or the pad function on a gesture or key). Keys 1-4 start 5, 10, 15 or 25 minutes; turn for a minute more or less; click to start / pause; key 6 resets; key 7 switches to the stopwatch (key 8 there takes a lap). Focus (key 8) runs 25 minutes of work and 5 of break, round and round, with the busy light on while you work. Hold the encoder to go back to your keys: the time left shows on the top row and the timer keeps going. When it ends the pad beeps, flashes the lights and says so; any key, turn or click stops it.
  • Counter: TOOLS → Counter. A tally (keys +1, -1, +10, Reset, Type it; turning counts too), and every counter your Number keys use (Prev / Next).
  • Number keys (Keys → Action → Number): each press types the counter's next number, 1, 2, 3…: line numbers, item numbers. In a CSV, {NEXT LINE}. Start again from 1 on the Counter screen (Reset).
  • Date / time keys (Keys → Action → Date / time): types today's date or the time, in the format you pick. In a CSV, {DATE} or {TIME}. Days from today types a date ahead or back (in a CSV, {DATE MM/DD/YYYY +180}). The pad learns the time from this page (or the phone app) when it connects; until then it says CLOCK NOT SET.
  • Busy light: TOOLS → Busy light (or the pad function): the lights turn red and BUSY shows on the top row.
  • Reminders: Settings → Reminders: a time, a note, and which days. The pad beeps and shows the note (it needs the clock, so a page must have connected since the pad was plugged in).

Spotify

Double click the encoder (by default) for the pad's Spotify screen: the song, the artist and the time, and eight keys: + (save to / remove from Liked Songs), Shuffle, Repeat (off, all, one), Here (play on this computer), then Prev, Play / Pause, Next and Mute. The encoder is the volume. Click to go back to your layer.

The page needs to be open and signed in to Spotify (the Spotify tab: your own free Spotify key, once; the account needs Premium). When Spotify plays on this computer, the keys and the volume are the computer's own media keys; anywhere else (your phone, a speaker), they go through Spotify. The page asks once whether a computer it sees is this one.

Older keymaps and backups

A pad with a keymap from FIRMWARE_03 / 04 (or older) shows Convert it. Open a folder… opens a backup of a pad's drive; Open a file… opens one keymap file. Either way the keymap opens here first, marked Not on the pad yet, with What changed. Nothing goes to the pad until Save to the pad, and the pad's previous keymap is kept in an old_keymap_date folder on it (see Files).

If something's wrong

  • "Couldn't save": the pad's drive is showing on the computer (TOOLS → Drive). Hide it again; the pad restarts.
  • The pad shows PROBLEMS at start-up: a file it couldn't read. The page's Files tab lets you look at and download it.
  • Icons don't appear in the search: the icon sets need the internet. Pictures already on keys don't.
  • The page can't connect: close other tabs and programs using the pad, unplug it, plug it back in.
